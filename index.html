AI & Tax

Dr Aleksandra Bal
4 June 2026

There is now an AI tool for almost every task.

That does not mean every task needs one.

Choosing the right tool matters more than choosing the newest one.

AI will give you a confident answer to almost any question.

Whether it should is a different question.

Every AI tool has limits. Not every user knows where they are.

Every new technology starts with limits.

LLMs are not search engines, and they are not databases of verified facts.

Judging them as if they were is the wrong test.

The headlines about AI layoffs are hard to miss.

Every transformative technology rearranges work — it does not only erase it.

Every new technology invents new tax problems.

 

Sometimes the thing you're afraid of becomes your next client.

Don't build agents, build skills

Two engineers from Anthropic asked:

if you needed help with your tax return, would you choose:

  • an AI assistant
  • a human experienced tax professional who's been doing this for years?

Almost everyone picks the human.
